I.

The shift

A growing share of decisions no longer begins with a search, but with a question.

ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude answer questions directly — and choose for themselves which sources their answers are built on. Whoever isn't among the sources isn't part of the answer either.

For organizations, this means visibility is no longer measured in positions on a results page, but in how often you're cited, and in what contexts.2

Hovedkilde works on one thing: making your organization the source these systems build their answers on.

V.

Frequently asked questions

What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

Generative engine optimization is the work of making an organization a source that AI answer engines read, understand and cite. Where search engine optimization is about placement on a results page, GEO is about being part of the answer itself from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude.

How does Hovedkilde measure visibility in AI-generated answers?

We measure how often the organization is cited in answers to a defined set of decision-questions from the sector, across four answer engines. The method is documented and verifiable, and the measurement is repeated each quarter so that development can be followed over time.

Does Hovedkilde guarantee that we'll be cited?

No. We document visibility as a measurable frequency — we never promise it. The work consists of structuring the source base so answer engines can reproduce it, and of reporting progress openly at leadership level.
